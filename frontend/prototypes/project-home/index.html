RESEARCH REPORT · SAVED IN Puzzle Discovery

What keeps puzzle players engaged?

A qualitative look at the play patterns and product choices described in this report.

USAndroid24 Oct 2026Source status: unverified

Research conclusion

The report describes several ways puzzle experiences can encourage players to return, including clear progression, satisfying interactions and new goals. These observations are report-supplied and have not been independently verified by PO Hub.

Findings

Progression gives players a reason to continue

The report points to visible goals and a steady sense of progress. See the original report for supporting passages and sources.

Fresh challenges help renew interest

New level ideas and periodic content are described as ways to keep the experience feeling active.

What remains unknown

This report does not establish which specific feature has the greatest effect, or provide comparable retention measurements.
